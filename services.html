Regulatory Affairs

Market entry strategy through to post-market obligations, with India and CDSCO as our primary depth and EU, US and other markets alongside.

  • CDSCO MDR 2017 licensing across manufacturing, import, wholesale and test
  • EU MDR 745 and IVDR 746 technical files and CE marking
  • US FDA 510(k), PMA and QMSR compliance
  • WHO PQ, PMDA, ANVISA, Health Canada, BIS certification
  • Clinical investigation and performance evaluation
  • Post-market surveillance, PSUR and licence renewals

  • Product quality, supplier quality and warehouse quality management
  • Internal audit programmes and CAPA systems

  • Multi-site harmonisation

Supporting Practice

What Makes Compliance Hold

Approvals are won once. Staying compliant depends on whether the equipment is qualified, the processes are controlled, the systems are digital and the people are competent.

⚙️

Equipment Qualification

A URS-anchored qualification chain where every test traces back to a stated requirement, covering data integrity and cybersecurity alongside mechanical performance.

  • User Requirement Specification before enquiry
  • DQ, IQ, OQ and PQ protocols and execution
  • Computer system validation to GAMP 5 and 21 CFR Part 11
  • Retrospective qualification and requalification

  • Business and process KPI definition and governance

Digitalization
